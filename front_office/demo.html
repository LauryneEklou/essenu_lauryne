Understanding French Labor Law

Legal 2025-01-15 by Marie Dupont

Introduction to Labor Rights

French labor law is one of the most comprehensive in Europe, providing extensive protections for workers. This guide covers the fundamental aspects every employee should understand.

Key Points to Remember

  • Every employee has the right to a written contract
  • The standard work week is 35 hours
  • Minimum wage (SMIC) is adjusted annually
  • Paid vacation is guaranteed at 5 weeks per year
"Workers' rights are fundamental human rights that must be protected and respected."
— International Labour Organization

Types of Employment Contracts

There are several types of employment contracts in France:

  1. CDI (Contrat à Durée Indéterminée) - Permanent contract
  2. CDD (Contrat à Durée Déterminée) - Fixed-term contract
  3. Interim (Contrat de Travail Temporaire) - Temporary work

Example Calculation

// Monthly salary calculation
const hourlyRate = 11.65; // SMIC 2025
const hoursPerWeek = 35;
const weeksPerMonth = 52 / 12;

const monthlySalary = hourlyRate * hoursPerWeek * weeksPerMonth;
console.log(`Monthly SMIC: €${monthlySalary.toFixed(2)}`);

For more information, consult with a labor law specialist or visit the official government website.
